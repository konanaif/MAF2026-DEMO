{% endblock %} {% block body_content %} DEE-MC applies DEE fairness regularization to a GLocal-K matrix-completion recommender. The supplied model combines two GLocal-K checkpoints with stochastic weight averaging (SWA) and a matrix-factorization (MF) predictor.
- Test RMSE: Predicted versus observed 1–5 ratings on the 100,020 test entries
- DEE: Sum of four absolute gaps between the overall positive prediction rate and the rates for sex × genre-group combinations, over the original sensitive-item × all-user population. Positive means predicted rating ≥ 3
- Performance metrics, Classification metrics: the 18 MAF group-fairness metrics computed on the held-out ratings that users actually gave, with predicted rating ≥ 3.0 as the positive label and sex as the protected attribute (privileged: Male, unprivileged: Female)
